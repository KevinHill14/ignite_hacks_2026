HIST 280: Modern Europe, 1789-1945
Fall 2026 - Department of History

Instructor: Dr. S. Bergmann
Lecture: Wednesdays 18:00-21:00, JOR-402
Term begins the week of September 8, 2026. Reading week falls October 12-16.

COURSE MATERIALS

A printed course pack is required and is available from the campus
copy centre for $32.00. Please purchase it during the first week of
classes; the copy centre does not reprint after week three.

Five documentary films are assigned over the term. These may be
borrowed free from the library reserve desk, or streamed for $4.99
each if you prefer to watch at home. Streaming is optional.

SCHEDULE

Week 1  - Introduction: the Old Regime
Week 2  - Revolution and Terror
Week 3  - Response paper 1 due at the start of lecture
Week 4  - Napoleon and the European order
Week 5  - Industrialization; MIDTERM EXAMINATION held in the second
          hour of lecture this week
Week 6  - Nationalism (no lecture - reading week)
Week 7  - The class immediately following reading week: 1848 and its
          aftermath. Response paper 2 due.
Week 8  - Imperialism
Week 9  - The First World War
Week 10 - Versailles and the interwar years. Research essay due at
          the start of lecture.
Week 11 - Fascism
Week 12 - The Second World War
Week 13 - Last week of classes: review and course conclusion

The final examination is scheduled by the registrar during the
December examination period.

GRADING
Response papers (2)   20%
Midterm               25%
Research essay        25%
Final examination     30%
