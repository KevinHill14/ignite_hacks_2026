PHIL 101 - Introduction to Philosophy - Fall 2026

Instructor: Dr. R. Vance (rvance@example.edu). Tuesdays and Thursdays, 15:00-16:20, AR-201.

This course surveys central problems in philosophy: knowledge, mind, free will, and ethics. Readings are posted to the course portal each week; there is no textbook to purchase. Assessment is by two short papers, a midterm held in October, and a final examination during the December examination period. Exact dates will be announced in class. Office hours by appointment.
